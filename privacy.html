Trade Alerts Privacy Policy

Last updated: August 14, 2026

Information Used

Trade Alerts uses the phone numbers of authorized recipients and information necessary to generate account-activity notifications. The service may process limited information about trading or account events solely for the purpose of generating requested alerts.

How Information Is Used

Recipient phone numbers are used only to deliver Trade Alerts SMS notifications and administer the SMS program. Trade Alerts does not use recipient information to send advertising or promotional messages.

SMS Consent and Phone Numbers

Mobile phone numbers, SMS opt-in data, and SMS consent are not sold, rented, or shared with third parties or affiliates for marketing or promotional purposes.

Service Providers

Information may be processed by service providers only as necessary to operate the alert service, such as telecommunications and SMS delivery providers. These providers are used to deliver the requested service and not for independent marketing by Trade Alerts.

Opting Out

Recipients can stop SMS messages at any time by replying STOP to (844) 946-2689. Reply HELP for SMS program assistance.

Data Minimization

Trade Alerts is intended for a limited set of authorized recipients. Information is limited to what is reasonably necessary to operate and administer the notification service.
