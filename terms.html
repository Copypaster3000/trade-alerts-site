Trade Alerts SMS Terms & Conditions

Last updated: August 14, 2026

Program Description

Trade Alerts is a private SMS notification service that sends authorized recipients automated notifications regarding trading and account activity on monitored financial and cryptocurrency accounts. The program is not used for advertising or promotional messaging.

Enrollment and Consent

Recipients enroll by texting START to (844) 946-2689. Trade Alerts then provides the program disclosures and asks the recipient to reply YES to confirm enrollment. Messages are sent only after this confirmation.

Message Frequency

Message frequency varies based on activity occurring on monitored accounts. There is no fixed number of messages per day or month.

Message and Data Rates

Message and data rates may apply according to the recipient's wireless plan. Carriers are not liable for delayed or undelivered messages.

Opt Out

Reply STOP to (844) 946-2689 at any time to stop receiving Trade Alerts messages. After opting out, the recipient will no longer receive program messages unless they enroll again.

Help

Reply HELP to (844) 946-2689 for help with the SMS program.

Privacy

Information associated with the SMS program is handled according to the Trade Alerts Privacy Policy.

Program Availability

Trade Alerts may modify, suspend, or discontinue the SMS program. Delivery is dependent on third-party telecommunications and messaging services and is not guaranteed.
