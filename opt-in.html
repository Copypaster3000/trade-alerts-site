Trade Alerts SMS Opt-In

This page documents the complete keyword-based SMS opt-in campaign for Trade Alerts.

How to opt in: Text START to (844) 946-2689. Trade Alerts is a private, non-marketing account-activity notification service for authorized recipients.

SMS Opt-In Flow

Step 1 — Recipient Texts START to (844) 946-2689.
Step 2 — Trade Alerts Welcome to Trade Alerts. You'll receive automated SMS notifications about trading and account activity on monitored accounts. Message frequency varies based on account activity. Msg & data rates may apply. Reply HELP for help or STOP to opt out. Terms: https://copypaster3000.github.io/trade-alerts-site/terms.html Privacy: https://copypaster3000.github.io/trade-alerts-site/privacy.html Reply YES to confirm enrollment.
Step 3 — Recipient Replies YES to confirm enrollment.
Step 4 — Trade Alerts You're enrolled in Trade Alerts. You'll receive automated trading and account-activity notifications. Message frequency varies. Reply HELP for help or STOP to unsubscribe.

HELP Response

If a recipient texts HELP to (844) 946-2689, the automated response is:

Trade Alerts Trade Alerts: You're receiving automated trading and account-activity notifications from Trade Alerts. For help with the SMS program, reply HELP. To stop receiving messages, reply STOP.
