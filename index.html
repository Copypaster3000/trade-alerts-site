Trade Alerts

Trade Alerts is a private automated SMS notification service for authorized recipients. It sends notifications regarding trading and account activity on monitored financial and cryptocurrency accounts.

This service is not used for advertising or promotional messaging. SMS messages are sent only to recipients who explicitly opt in and confirm enrollment.

SMS Program

To enroll, text START to (844) 946-2689 and follow the confirmation instructions.

Message frequency varies based on account activity. Message and data rates may apply. Reply STOP to opt out or HELP for help.
